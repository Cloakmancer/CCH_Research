### Findings

Two results that constrain the methodology rather than describing it.

* **λ_U cannot be resolved from a single 40-year record, and this is a scope limit on Next
  Action 4.** Estimating λ_U at u = 0.95 uses only the n(1−u) = 2 observations above the cutoff,
  giving a binomial standard error of ±0.35 against a Gumbel-vs-Gaussian gap of 0.20 — the error
  bar is 1.7× the quantity it must resolve. At u = 0.99 a 40-year record contains 0.4 expected
  points, usually none. Roughly 1,000 years of annual maxima are needed to bring the u = 0.99
  standard error to ±0.156, still comparable to the 0.315 gap.

  The plan already said "do not select on AIC alone" and "empirical λ_U(u) over u ∈ [0.9, 0.99]".
  That diagnostic stands, but it cannot be expected to *decide* between families on one station.
  Family choice has to rest on physical argument (peak and volume come out of the same storm),
  on regional pooling to buy effective record length, or on carrying families through and
  reporting the spread — which makes **Next Action 7 load-bearing rather than a nicety**, and is
  worth deciding before the results section is drafted rather than after.

* **The margin/λ_U relationship in the supplied bridge note needed sharpening.** The note said an
  incorrectly light-tailed marginal "will understate λ". λ_U is a property of the copula alone and
  is invariant to the margins — that invariance *is* Sklar's theorem, so a marginal choice cannot
  move the true λ_U. What is true is two separate things: the *estimate* is contaminated if the
  margins are parametric (IFM), which is the standing argument for semiparametric fitting on
  empirical ranks; and the design value in physical units depends on the margin regardless, worth
  1.58× across four candidate margins at u = 0.99 in `day2-margin-vs-copula.png`. The conclusion
  the note drew is right; the mechanism is estimation and back-transformation, not λ_U itself
  moving. Recorded because a reviewer who knows copulas would read the original phrasing as a
  misunderstanding of Sklar, and the argument does not need the overclaim.

## 2026-08-04

### Topic

Fit GEV to Nepal daily-rainfall annual maxima for ξ — the Day 1 open thread, taught and executed
as a single session rather than deferred again.

### Knowledge Gained

* GEV is fit to block maxima (Fisher–Tippett–Gnedenko), never to the raw series. The daily
  zero-atom (65–80% dry days per station) lives in the daily data and does not reach the fit
  once annual maxima are taken — no station's 41-year annual maximum came anywhere near zero.
  The zero-inflation/mixture-likelihood machinery this question suggested is real, but one step
  upstream: it's needed for fitting F(x) to the *whole* daily series (the pseudo-observations
  question), not for the GEV/AMS step.
* What genuinely needs deciding at the AMS-extraction step is a missing-data completeness rule
  (used ≥90% of days present per year, i.e. ≥330/365) — didn't bind for the 5 stations used
  (all 100% complete) but will for the next, less-clean batch.
* MLE instability in ξ at n≈41 is real and asymmetric, not a coding error: parametric-bootstrap
  95% CIs ranged from (−0.25, 0.28) at Kathmandu to (0.09, 5.0) at Surkhet, the latter pulled by
  a single outlier year (423.1 mm vs. next-highest 281.2 mm). Same shape as the Day 2 finding on
  λ_U — short single-site records can't pin down tail behaviour precisely, univariate or bivariate.

* Verified numerically, not asserted: Kendall's τ is exactly invariant under a monotonic
  transform of one margin (log Q, Q³ — τ = 0.733 in all three cases on the same 6-point toy
  dataset) while Pearson r moves (0.879 → 0.902 → 0.816). This is the concrete reason Next
  Action 2 fits τ before a copula parameter rather than r — Sklar's decomposition only asks a
  question rank correlation can answer without contamination from the margins' shapes.
* Section-level "card" layout is achievable site-wide with no markdown changes: a small JS pass
  (`sectionize.js`) groups each H2 and its following siblings into a wrapper div at load time.
  Material's right-hand TOC, scrollspy and search all key off heading `id`s via
  `getElementById`/anchor href, not DOM depth, so nesting headings one level deeper for styling
  purposes is safe — checked directly (all H2 ids from a 13-section page still resolved from the
  generated TOC links) rather than assumed.
