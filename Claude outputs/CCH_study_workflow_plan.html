Wk 5–6
Copulas deep
Read
Serinaldi & Kilsby 2015 — full paper. Read critically. Note every critique that applies to what you built in Week 4.

Bevacqua et al. 2019 — Methods section.
Non-stationarity
Hands-on
Fit all four copula families (Gumbel, Clayton, Frank, Gaussian) to your dataset. Use AIC/BIC to select best. Compute tail dependence λ_U. Run Genest-Rivest goodness-of-fit test. Plot Kendall's K_C.
R / VineCopula
Output
Copula selection table (AIC, BIC, λ_U for each family) + selected copula level curves at T = 10, 50, 100, 200 yr.
Results table

DualSPHysics
Hands-on

Goal: connect the pillars. Use SPH to generate synthetic flood scenarios. Use those scenarios as input to your AI model. Frame a compound hazard problem using copulas and solve a piece of it with each tool.

SPH → data
Read
Ward et al. 2018 — full paper. Note how they handle data scarcity in South Asian systems.

AghaKouchak et al. 2020 — Section on data-scarce regions.
Compound flood
Hands-on
Build a custom SPH dam-break scenario with a simple downstream obstacle (a wall or building). Run 5 variants at different initial water heights. Extract peak velocity and inundation depth at the obstacle for each run. You now have a synthetic dataset of (input, output) pairs.
DualSPHysics
Output
CSV of 5 SPH scenarios: (initial depth, peak velocity, max inundation depth, force on obstacle). This is your surrogate model training dataset — small but real.
