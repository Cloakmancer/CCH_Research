# Statistics Study Log

A parallel, deliberately elementary statistics track (StatQuest playlist plus worked
exercises), kept separate from the main CCH methodology work. Its purpose is scaffolding:
every session ends with an explicit **bridge** — what the concept implies for compound
cascading hazard analysis. The concept itself is standard undergraduate material; the bridge
is the part that belongs to this project.

Working files live outside this repository, in `Stat_Study/` (coursework, practice problems,
notes, datasets). Only the concept and its bridge are logged here.

Format per entry: **Concept → What it actually says → Bridge to CCH → Open thread.** (Day 4
departs from this — worked-reference format instead of a log entry, with its own numbered
sections and the practice problems folded in rather than a separate page. Noted on the page
itself.)
